US **immigration** officers have taken at least four children from the city of Columbia Heights, Minnesota, according to school leaders and a family lawyer. The children include two 17-year-olds, a 10-year-old, and a five-year-old boy named Liam.

Liam and his father were taken to a family holding center in Texas. The family’s lawyer said they are in the US legally and are waiting for a **court hearing**. The government said the father was in the country illegally but gave no details.

The superintendent of the local school district described what has been happening in the area.

"ICE agents have been **roaming** our neighborhoods, circling our schools, following our buses, coming into our parking lots multiple times, and taking our kids," she said.

Federal officers have increased immigration actions in the Minneapolis area as part of President Donald Trump’s immigration policy. This has made many people in the community feel afraid and unsafe. Some residents have organized groups to warn neighbors when officers are nearby.

According to **witnesses**, officers took Liam’s father after he and the child returned home from **preschool**. Several adults offered to take care of the child, including school officials and neighbors, but officers did not allow it. A school board leader said the child was used to trick the boy’s mother into coming outside. When asked if this was true, she said, "Correct."

Vice President JD Vance **defended** the officers’ actions at a **press conference**.

"What are they supposed to do? Are they supposed to let a five-year-old child freeze to death? Are they not supposed to arrest an illegal alien?" he said.

Community leaders said the events have deeply affected families and children, and they believe children should be safe and in school with their classmates.
