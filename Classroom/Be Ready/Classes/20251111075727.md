### English grammar: Third conditional practice

The teacher guided LUIZ HENRIQUE through exercises focusing on the third conditional structure.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the structure requires "would have + past participle" in one clause and "if + had + past participle" in the other clause
    - LUIZ HENRIQUE: Practiced translating sentences like "I would have called him if I had known his number"
    - Be: Provided corrections when LUIZ HENRIQUE made mistakes with the structure
- Conclusion
    
    - The third conditional is used to talk about hypothetical situations in the past
    - The correct structure is "would have + past participle" and "if + had + past participle"
