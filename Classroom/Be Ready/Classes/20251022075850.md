### English grammar: Present perfect vs. simple past tense

Extensive discussion and practice on when to use present perfect tense versus simple past tense in English.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained common misconceptions about when to use "have" + past participle
    - Be: Demonstrated that using time markers like "yesterday" or "last week" requires simple past
    - Be: Taught that certain words like "before," "already," "just," "recently," and time periods with "for" typically require present perfect
    - Luana: Practiced forming sentences with both tenses
    - Be: Corrected Luana's usage and provided explanations for the correct forms
- Conclusion
    
    - Present perfect (have + past participle) is used with words like "before," "already," "just," "recently," and "for [time period]"
    - Simple past is used with specific time markers like "yesterday," "last week," etc.
    - Learning happens when the brain makes connections, not just through focused study
