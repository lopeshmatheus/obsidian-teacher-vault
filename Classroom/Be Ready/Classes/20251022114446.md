### Using "near" to describe proximity

Class focused on expressing location relationships using "near."

- Details
    
    - Structure taught: "There is a [place] near my [location]"
    - Be: Emphasized that "near" is followed directly by the location without additional prepositions (not "near to" or "near from")
    - Practice examples: "There is a bank near my building," "There is a hospital near the church," "There are two museums in my neighborhood"
    - Students practiced using "the" with specific places and "my" with personal locations
- Conclusion
    
    - Students learned to use "near" correctly without extra prepositions
    - Practiced combining location vocabulary with proximity expressions
