## Topic 1: Grammar Practice

The instructor focused on several grammatical structures that Luiz was struggling with. First was the "need + gerund" structure used to express impersonal requirements, such as "The equipment needs replacing" instead of "We need to replace the equipment." This structure is particularly useful in professional contexts when discussing objects rather than people's actions. The instructor provided examples like "My room needs cleaning" and "The car needs fixing" to reinforce this pattern. Another focus was the expression "get to know" versus simply "know," emphasizing that "get to know" indicates the process of becoming familiar with something new. The instructor also reviewed the difference between "there are" for expressing existence and forms of "have" for possession, correcting Luiz's tendency to confuse these structures. Finally, they practiced past perfect tense with examples like "She had come here before I came here," highlighting how this tense shows the sequence of past events.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: How can I say "A gente precisa ou, OS equipamentos, preciso... O precisamos mundarios equipamentos"?

Luiz Henrique: We needed to replace the equipment.

Be: Very good. But there is a way of expressing this idea that can sound more professional and more impersonal... the equipment needs replacing. Have you ever heard this structure before?

Be: How can I say "EU coinha su novos lugares"?

Luiz Henrique: I... no... new place?

Be: If you say "I know new places," you are talking about something in the present. But I want to say the fact of beginning to know. When I travel, I get to know new places.

## Topic 2: Business Travel Discussion

The class began with a conversation about Luiz's current business trip to Belo Horizonte. Luiz explained that his role involves inspecting buildings and equipment at different company branches, taking pictures, writing reports, and escalating situations that need attention. He mentioned having traveled to various locations including Argentina and Hawaii for work. While Luiz acknowledged enjoying seeing new places and cultures during these trips, he described business travel as a "lone life" since he typically travels alone, eats at restaurants by himself, and focuses primarily on work. He shared a recent experience of feeling particularly lonely while dining alone surrounded by families celebrating birthdays. Luiz also explained that this current trip was somewhat unexpected, as his boss had visited the location a week prior and determined that Luiz needed to go fix some issues, giving him only about two weeks' notice.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: Is it common for you to travel on business?

Luiz Henrique: I traveled to Argentina, Hawaii... I need to look for the area, or the machine, or the equipment...

Be: And do you like to travel for business?

Luiz Henrique: Yeah, but I think it's a lone life. I generally travel alone. And I go to restaurants alone. And I work and return to hotel and work.

Be: I imagine that in the beginning it was a little bit more exciting?

Luiz Henrique: Yeah, but mainly if I didn't prepare about that. Sometimes my boss says "You need to go there."

## Topic 3: Reading Comprehension on Failed Marriage Proposals

The main lesson focused on a reading about two failed marriage proposals. The first story was about Alex, whose girlfriend Chloe proposed to him using a bracelet with letter beads spelling "Will you marry me?" Alex, who had previously told Chloe he didn't want to get married or have children, pretended not to understand the message by rearranging the letters. Chloe explained that February 29th (which occurs once every four years) is traditionally when women can propose to men. Despite the rejection, they remained together. The second story featured Emma, whose ex-boyfriend Tom proposed to her at a cathedral with champagne, flowers, and a necklace (not a ring). Emma had already ended the relationship, but Tom hoped the proposal would solve their problems. She rejected him in front of Japanese tourists who were taking photos, and they walked to the station together where Tom admitted his reasoning. The class discussed both stories, with Luiz expressing that it's better to align expectations before proposing and that breaking up early is better than accepting a proposal that doesn't align with one's desires.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: What do you think about the way Alex and Emma behaved?

Luiz Henrique: I think it's okay, but in my opinion, it's better to align before this intention.

Be: Do you feel sorry for Chloe and Tom?

Luiz Henrique: No, because in my opinion, I think it's better to break up a situation in the beginning. Because if they had accepted the proposals, probably in the future it's not a good relation.
