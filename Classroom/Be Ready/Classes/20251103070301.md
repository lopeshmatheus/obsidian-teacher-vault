### English grammar: Articles and nouns

Be explained the proper use of articles with nouns in English.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained that countable nouns need either "a/an" or to be plural
    - Be: Demonstrated examples with words like "dog," "manager," and "doctor"
    - Luiz: Practiced forming sentences with proper article usage
    - Be: Clarified that some nouns (like "coffee" and "money") don't require articles
- Conclusion
    
    - In English, countable nouns must either have "a/an" or be plural
    - Uncountable nouns (coffee, money) don't require articles
    - This differs from Portuguese grammar rules

### Verb forms: Manage, managing, manager

Be helped Luiz understand different forms of the same word.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the difference between "manage" (verb), "managing" (gerund/present participle), and "manager" (noun)
    - Luiz: Practiced using each form in sentences
    - Be: Corrected Luiz's usage and provided examples
- Conclusion
    
    - Different forms serve different grammatical functions
    - "I manage" (present tense verb)
    - "I am managing" (present continuous)
    - "I am a manager" (noun with article)
