## Key takeaways

- The class focused on English language learning, particularly past simple tense and irregular verbs
- Harrison Ford was used as an example to discuss celebrities helping people
- Students practiced forming past tense verbs and sentence structures
- The instructor emphasized learning through practice rather than memorizing rules
- Students worked on vocabulary related to transportation verbs (fly, drive)

## Discussed topics

### English language warm-up

The instructor began with basic conversation practice to get students comfortable speaking English.

- Details
    
    - Be: Asked students what they did today
    - Rafael: Responded that he worked today
    - Larissa: Mentioned she went to work at 7 o'clock and returned at 5 p.m.
- Conclusion
    
    - Students demonstrated basic conversation skills in English
    - The instructor provided corrections and encouragement

### Present continuous tense review

The instructor reviewed the present continuous tense structure with students.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the structure of present continuous (I am + verb+ing)
    - Larissa: Practiced forming sentences like "I am working now"
    - Rafael: Practiced with the verb "play" to form "I am playing"
- Conclusion
    
    - Students learned to distinguish between present simple and present continuous
    - The instructor emphasized the importance of using the correct verb form with "am"

### Harrison Ford discussion

The class discussed Harrison Ford as an example of a celebrity who helps people.

- Details
    
    - Be: Showed a photo of Harrison Ford and asked students to identify him
    - Rafael: Recognized him as Han Solo from Star Wars
    - Larissa: Estimated his age at around 70 years old
    - Rafael: Mentioned Ford appeared in Star Wars and Indiana Jones
- Conclusion
    
    - Students practiced describing a person in English
    - The discussion served as context for the upcoming grammar lesson

### Video about Harrison Ford helping people

Students watched a video about Harrison Ford using his helicopter to rescue someone.

- Details
    
    - Be: Played a video showing how Harrison Ford rescued a hiker
    - The video explained that Ford offers his pilot services to local authorities
    - Students answered true/false questions about the video content
- Conclusion
    
    - Ford rescued a dehydrated hiker who was lost in the wilderness
    - He flew her to the hospital in his helicopter
    - The woman called the police, who then called Ford for help

### Past simple tense and irregular verbs

The instructor taught students about past simple tense, focusing on regular and irregular verbs.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the two types of verbs in past simple: regular (-ed) and irregular
    - Rafael: Practiced with verbs like "read" (past: read)
    - Larissa: Practiced with verbs like "forgive" (past: forgave)
    - Be: Demonstrated how to form negatives with "didn't" + infinitive
- Conclusion
    
    - Regular verbs add -ed in past tense
    - Irregular verbs change form in unpredictable ways
    - The instructor advised learning through exposure rather than memorizing rules

### Transportation verbs

The class learned specific verbs related to transportation.

- Details
    
    - Be: Taught the structure "fly someone somewhere" meaning to transport by air
    - Larissa: Practiced with "fly the woman to work"
    - Rafael: Used "I flew the woman to school"
    - Be: Explained similar structures with other transportation verbs: drive (car), ride (motorcycle)
- Conclusion
    
    - "Fly someone somewhere" means to transport someone by air
    - "Drive someone somewhere" means to transport someone by car
    - These verbs directly incorporate the mode of transportation

## Action items

- Students
    
    - Practice past simple tense with both regular and irregular verbs
    - Review the transportation verbs (fly, drive, ride)
    - Continue practicing present continuous tense
    - Watch more English content with subtitles to learn naturally
- Be (Instructor)
    
    - Provide more examples of irregular verbs for practice
    - Continue using real-world contexts for grammar instruction
