### English grammar: Causative structures

Be taught Luana about causative structures with "have something done" in English.

- Details
    
    - Be: Introduced the structure through examples like "I had my car fixed" vs "I fixed my car"
    - Be: Explained the difference between doing something yourself and having someone else do it
    - Luana: Practiced with examples like "I had my apartment painted" and "I had my pictures taken"
    - Be: Provided feedback and corrections on Luana's sentences
- Conclusion
    
    - The causative structure "have something done" is used when someone else performs an action for you
    - This structure doesn't have a direct translation in Portuguese, making it challenging for learners

### Past tense structures in English

Be and Luana reviewed different past tense structures in English and when to use each form.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the difference between simple past (I worked) and present perfect (I have worked)
    - Be: Discussed how time expressions influence which tense to use
    - Luana: Recalled her previous knowledge about using simple past with specific time expressions
    - Be: Suggested learning which expressions go with which tense through practice rather than rules
    - Luana: Practiced with examples like "I have lived in England for 2 years" vs "I lived in England"
- Conclusion
    
    - Time expressions like "twice," "recently," and "for two years" typically pair with present perfect
    - Specific past time expressions like "yesterday" and "last year" pair with simple past
    - Learning which expressions go with which tense is more effective than memorizing general rules
