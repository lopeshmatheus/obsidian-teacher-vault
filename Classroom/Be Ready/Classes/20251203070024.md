## Topic 1: Homework Review and Grammar Practice

The lesson began with a review of homework assignments, focusing on exercises from November 24th that Luiz found particularly challenging. These exercises covered vocabulary related to crime and safety, including terms like "burglar" and "robber." The teacher guided Luiz through each question, providing explanations and corrections. They also reviewed another exercise on verb patterns with prepositions, such as "refuse to go," "admit stealing," "regret saying," and "threaten to tell." The teacher emphasized the importance of using the correct prepositions with specific verbs, noting patterns like "insist on" and "accuse of." Additionally, they practiced the past perfect continuous tense, distinguishing between "has been" and "had been" in different contexts, particularly with time markers like "all day" and "for two hours."

### Relevant Q&A

Be: What if I say "Eu estou muito cansado"? I am very tired because...

LUIZ HENRIQUE: I'm very tired because I worked... very much yesterday.

Be: When we say "all day," it's one of the special words like "recently," "twice," "since," etc. The moment you say something in the past, it changes from "I have been working all day" to "I had been working all day."

## Topic 2: Email Writing Correction

Luiz had written a professional email in English without assistance and requested feedback from the teacher. The email was addressed to colleagues named Janice and Noel regarding access issues for a user. The teacher provided specific corrections, noting that "nowadays" should be written as one word and suggesting changes to the phrase "could we help us" to "could we help him" for clarity. The teacher also offered alternative expressions for email openings, suggesting "I hope this email finds you well" as a variation to "I hope you're doing well" to add variety to professional correspondence. This segment demonstrated practical application of English in a workplace context and highlighted common phrases used in professional communication.

### Relevant Q&A

LUIZ HENRIQUE: I would like if you could correct for me... my mistakes, or if there are some issues, or some wrong keywords.

Be: Just a tip for improvement - "I hope you're doing well" is perfect, nothing wrong with that, but one other expression that I see people using in email, especially corporate email, is "I hope this email finds you well." It's just an alternative, but both are good.

## Topic 3: Listening Comprehension Exercise

The class worked on a listening comprehension exercise featuring two unusual news stories. The first story was about "Vindigo," a blue wine produced in Spain by a French entrepreneur after French companies refused to get involved. The wine costs 12 euros per bottle and is described as ideal for summer, with aromas of cherry, blackberry, and passion fruit. The second story concerned an Egyptian zoo that denied painting a donkey with black stripes to make it look like a zebra. A student named Mahmoud Sahan noticed the deception, pointing out differences between zebras and donkeys, including the color of the nose and the pattern of stripes. The teacher guided Luiz through comprehension questions, pausing the audio when necessary to clarify details and vocabulary.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: Where does this blue or Vindigo color come from?

LUIZ HENRIQUE: I understood it's a new wine, but I...

Be: It's from the red grape's skin. The wine is passed through this.

## Topic 4: Personal Conversation About Fruits and Weather

During a break from structured exercises, the teacher and student engaged in casual conversation about personal preferences regarding fruits and weather. Luiz expressed his preference for sour fruits like mango and kiwi, and mentioned that he freezes grapes for a few hours to create a healthy ice cream alternative. The teacher shared a personal anecdote about being allergic to hot weather, which causes skin rashes. Luiz mentioned that he lives in a valley about an hour from São Paulo, where temperatures range from 10°C in winter to 35-36°C in summer, and expressed his preference for hot weather. This conversation provided authentic practice in expressing personal preferences and experiences while building rapport.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: Do you have a favorite fruit?

LUIZ HENRIQUE: That depends on the day... I eat all fruit, but I like manga, I like kiwi.

Be: When do you prefer? Like, when it's too cold, or when it's too hot?

LUIZ HENRIQUE: Too hot. I like it.
