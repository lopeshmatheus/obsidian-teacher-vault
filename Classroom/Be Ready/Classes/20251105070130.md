### Third conditional structure

The instructor focused on teaching the third conditional structure, which expresses hypothetical situations in the past.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained that the third conditional consists of two parts: "would have + past participle" and "if + had + past participle"
    - Be: Emphasized that when one part of the structure is used, the other part naturally follows
    - Be: Demonstrated that "would have" corresponds to "teria" in Portuguese
    - Be: Showed that "if I had" corresponds to "se eu tivesse" in Portuguese
    - Luiz Henrique: Initially struggled with the structure, often using "should have" instead of "would have"
    - Be: Pointed out that Portuguese sometimes uses simpler forms (like "se eu fosse" instead of "se eu tivesse sido") while English maintains the consistent "if I had been" structure
- Conclusion
    
    - The third conditional follows a consistent pattern: "would have + past participle" in one clause and "if + had + past participle" in the other
    - The structure needs to become automatic rather than requiring conscious grammatical analysis
    - By the end of the session, Luiz Henrique showed improvement in using the structure correctly

### Practice exercises

The instructor provided extensive practice with the third conditional through various examples and translations.

- Details

    - Be: Practiced with phrases like "Eles teriam sido mais felizes" (They would have been happier)
    - Be: Had Luiz Henrique translate phrases like "Se eles tivessem falado comigo, eu teria emprestado dinheiro" (If they had talked to me, I would have lent money)
    - Be: Pointed out differences between Portuguese and English expressions, such as "se eu soubesse" sometimes translating to "if I had known"
    - Luiz Henrique: Gradually improved his responses, becoming more comfortable with the structure
