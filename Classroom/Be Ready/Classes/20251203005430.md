## Overview

This class session focused on English grammar practice, vocabulary development, and conversational skills. The teacher (Be) worked with the student (Luana) on completing missed homework assignments, reviewing conditional structures, and exploring verb patterns with infinitives and gerunds. The class combined structured grammar exercises with natural conversation about TV shows, allowing Luana to practice both formal language skills and casual English discussion.

### Key Concepts or Theories:

- Conditional structures (if clauses) with past perfect and modal verbs
- Verb patterns with infinitives and gerunds (offer to help, suggest going)
- Regular vs. irregular verbs in English
- Word choice and vocabulary related to actions and behaviors

### Important Questions Raised:

- How do English speakers determine which verb form (infinitive, gerund) follows specific verbs?
- Why are commonly used verbs in English often irregular?
- How can learners approach English grammar through pattern recognition rather than memorizing rules?

## Key Takeaways and Summary of Learning Objectives

- Understanding when to use different conditional structures in English (if I had saved money, I would have traveled)
- Recognizing patterns with verbs followed by infinitives or gerunds (offer to help vs. suggest going)
- Learning vocabulary related to actions and behaviors (admit, regret, threaten, convince)
- Practicing natural conversation skills through discussion of TV shows and entertainment
- Developing strategies for guessing correct forms when uncertain about grammar rules

## Topic 1: Conditional Structures Practice

The class began with Luana completing missed homework on conditional structures. They worked through exercises focusing on third conditional (if + past perfect + would have + past participle) and second conditional (if + past simple + would + base verb) structures. The teacher guided Luana through examples like "If I had saved money, I would have traveled" and "If I were you, I wouldn't worry about it." They also examined mixed conditionals and discussed how the structure changes depending on whether the condition refers to the past or present. The teacher emphasized pattern recognition rather than memorization of rules, encouraging Luana to identify the relationship between the two parts of conditional sentences.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: Why is "If I had anxiety if I drink coffee" correct?

Luana: I don't know, I don't remember.

Be: It's because "have" here is not the auxiliary verb but the main verb meaning "to possess." We're talking about something in the present: "I would have anxiety now if I drink coffee."

## Topic 2: Verb Patterns with Infinitives and Gerunds

The teacher introduced a section on verb patterns, focusing on which verbs are followed by infinitives and which by gerunds. They explored examples like "offer to help," "refuse to go," "agree to do," and "suggest going." The teacher explained that English grammar has fewer conjugation forms than Portuguese, making it important to learn which form follows specific verbs. They practiced with various verbs including "admit stealing," "promise to call," "threaten to tell," "advise someone to," "convince someone to," and "regret telling." The teacher emphasized that instead of memorizing rules, Luana should try different versions until finding the correct one, as English has limited verb forms compared to Portuguese.

### Relevant Q&A

Be: How can I say "sugerir ir"?

Luana: Suggest to go?

Be: No. What do you have left?

Luana: [After several attempts] Suggest going?

Be: Yes, because "to go" is just one version of the word. You can never say "to going" or "to went."

## Topic 3: Conversational Practice Through TV Show Discussion

The latter part of the class shifted to conversational practice, with Luana and the teacher discussing various TV shows including Vampire Diaries, The Originals, Vikings, and Game of Thrones. This allowed Luana to practice natural English conversation while expressing opinions and discussing plot points. They particularly focused on Game of Thrones, discussing character development, plot twists, and their shared disappointment with the final season. This conversation provided context for vocabulary practice, including words like "sword," "Iron Throne," and expressions like "have the same feeling/thinking." The teacher occasionally corrected pronunciation or word choice while maintaining the flow of conversation.

### Relevant Q&A

Luana: I don't forgive them for what they did to Daenerys.

Be: What do you think should have happened to Daenerys?

Luana: I don't think they had to put her in this crazy situation. They picked an area and made her just like her father. This doesn't even make sense.

Be: I think it could be okay to make her mad, but they should have prepared her better. She didn't give any signs that she was going to be crazy.
