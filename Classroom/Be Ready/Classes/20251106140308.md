### Do vs. Make collocations

The main focus of the class was teaching the difference between expressions using "do" and "make" in English.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained that there's no logical rule for when to use "do" or "make"
    - Be: Taught that students need to learn these as fixed expressions
    - anthony.silva: Practiced various expressions with guidance
- Expressions covered:
    
    - do housework (not "do houseworks")
    - do the cleaning
    - make the bed
    - make lunch/breakfast/dinner (but "have lunch/breakfast/dinner")
    - make a call
    - make a noise
    - do sport (not "do sports")
    - do exercise
    - do the vacuuming
    - make a mistake
    - do the washing/laundry
    - do a test (as a student) / make a test (as a teacher)
    - make friends
- Conclusion
    
    - These expressions need to be memorized as units rather than translated directly
    - Context is important for choosing the correct verb

### Present perfect vs. simple past

The teacher reviewed when to use present perfect vs. simple past tense.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained that "yet" in questions requires using "have" (present perfect)
    - Be: Demonstrated that "recently" typically requires present perfect
    - Be: Showed that specific time references (yesterday, last week) require simple past
    - anthony.silva: Practiced forming sentences with both tenses
- Conclusion
    
    - Use present perfect with "yet," "recently," and when focusing on the action itself
    - Use simple past when focusing on when something happened
    - The student needs more exposure to these patterns

### Vocabulary lesson on time of day

Brief discussion about specific English terms for different times of day.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained the terms "dawn" (first light before sunrise), "dusk" (last light after sunset), and "twilight" (period between)
    - Be: Connected "twilight" to the vampire movie series of the same name
- Conclusion
    
    - These specific time-of-day terms don't have exact equivalents in Portuguese
