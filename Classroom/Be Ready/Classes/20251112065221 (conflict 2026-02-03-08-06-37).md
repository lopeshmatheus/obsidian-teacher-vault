### Past Perfect Tense and Reported Speech

Grammar lesson on using past perfect tense in different contexts, particularly in reported speech.

- Details
    
    - Be: Explained that when reporting what someone said in the past, the tense shifts backward (present becomes past, past becomes past perfect)
    - Be: Demonstrated with examples like "I like tennis" becoming "He told me that he liked tennis"
    - LUIZ HENRIQUE: Practiced converting direct speech to reported speech with various examples
    - Be: Clarified that "will" in future tense becomes "was going to" in reported speech
- Conclusion
    
    - Present tense verbs shift to past tense in reported speech
    - Past tense verbs shift to past perfect (had + past participle) in reported speech
    - Future tense ("will") shifts to "was going to" in reported speech

### Third Conditional Sentences

Discussion and practice of third conditional sentences expressing hypothetical past situations.

- Details
    
    - Be: Reviewed sentences with "would have" + past participle and "if" + past perfect
    - LUIZ HENRIQUE: Translated examples to understand the meaning in Portuguese
    - Be: Emphasized the structure "I would have been very annoyed if you had spoken"
- Conclusion
    
    - Third conditional sentences express how things could have been different in the past
    - The structure follows: would have + past participle + if + had + past participle

Listening comprehension exercise about rejected marriage proposals.

- Details

    - LUIZ HENRIQUE: Identified the topic as proposals that were not accepted
    - Be: Played an audio about Alex and Chloe's story where she proposed to him on February 29th
    - LUIZ HENRIQUE: Learned about the tradition that women can propose on February 29th
- Conclusion
    
    - Alex had previously told Chloe he didn't want to get married or have children
    - Despite this, Chloe proposed with a bracelet spelling "Will you marry me?"
    - Alex avoided directly rejecting her by spelling other words with the letters
    - They remained together despite the failed proposal
